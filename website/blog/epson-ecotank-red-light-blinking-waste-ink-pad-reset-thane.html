Verified Failure Symptoms

Following diagnostics bench tests at our Manpada workshop lab in Thane, here are the most frequently verified failure patterns:

  • Power light is on, while Ink and Paper drop icons flash alternately in an orange/red rhythm.
  • Computer monitor shows 'Service Required: A printer's ink pad is at the end of its service life. Please contact Epson Support.'
  • Print jobs immediately bounce with no carriage movement or roller pickup.
  • Excess ink leaking from the rear bottom drainage casing of the printer onto your desk.

Bench-Tested DIY Triage Steps

Before bringing your hardware or equipment into our diagnostic center, perform these verified technician steps:

Warning Against Fake Scam Reset Tools

Never download random '.exe' keygen reset tools from unverified YouTube links. Most contain malware that overwrites the motherboard EEPROM with corrupted Russian or Chinese firmware, permanently bricking Wi-Fi functionality.

Step 1: Check Carriage Path for Paper Fragments

Turn off printer, lift the scanner unit, and check the carriage belt. If both lights were flashing simultaneously, a tiny scrap of paper or a staple may be blocking the carriage guide rail.

Step 2: Inspect Optical Encoder Film

Use a dry cotton swab to gently inspect the transparent plastic strip running behind the printhead. If ink mist has smudged the tick-marks, the optical sensor loses position calibration.

Frequently Asked Technical Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can I just reset the software counter without changing the sponges?

A: Doing so is a fire and property hazard! Once the counter reaches 100%, the internal waste reservoir is full of liquid ink. If reset without replacing the pads, liquid ink will overflow onto the main logic board power circuits.

Q: How long does waste ink pad replacement take at Big Byte Systems?

A: We complete sponge replacement and official counter recalibration in under 45 minutes at our Manpada workshop.
