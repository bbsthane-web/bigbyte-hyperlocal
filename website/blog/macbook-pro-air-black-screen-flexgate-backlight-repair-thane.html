📊 Diagnostic Decision Matrix: MacBook Black Screen vs Flexgate vs GPU

Shine a bright torch behind Apple logo. If faint desktop is visible = Backlight Circuit / Cable failure. If pitch black = GPU / Logic board.

Open display to 45°: Screen lights up? Open past 90°: Screen turns black or flickers? = Classic Flexgate ribbon cable fatigue.

Verified Failure Symptoms

Following diagnostics bench tests at our Manpada workshop lab in Thane, here are the most frequently verified failure patterns:

  • The MacBook boots, keyboard lights up, chime rings, and trackpad clicks, but the display remains totally black.
  • Display turns on when lid is half-open (45°), but completely cuts off when opened to a normal viewing angle (90°-120°).
  • Uneven 'stage lighting' (alternating bright and dark beams) appears along the bottom edge of the screen.
  • External monitor connected via USB-C / HDMI works perfectly with full resolution.

Bench-Tested DIY Triage Steps

Before bringing your hardware or equipment into our diagnostic center, perform these verified technician steps:

Step 1: Perform the Flashlight Diagnostic

Turn on your MacBook in a dark room and shine your phone flashlight directly against the glass at an angle. If you can see the faint outline of your desktop or login icons, your LCD panel is 100% fine—only the high-voltage backlight power line is severed.

Step 2: External Monitor Verification

Connect your MacBook to a TV or external monitor via Type-C adapter. If macOS boots normally, your Apple Silicon M1/M2/Intel CPU and GPU are healthy; the issue is localized strictly to the display hinge flex.

Step 3: Reset SMC / NVRAM (Intel Models Only)

On Intel MacBooks (2016-2020), shut down, hold Shift + Control + Option + Power for 10 seconds, then release and boot. (Note: On M1/M2/M3 Apple Silicon, SMC resets happen automatically during restart).
