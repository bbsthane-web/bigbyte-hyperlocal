1. Bench Diagnostics & Quotation Approval

  • Transparent Evaluation: All devices undergo initial diagnostic triage (multimeter short testing, thermal imaging, power rail measurement, or microscope inspection) to determine the exact component failure.
  • Prior Estimate Approval: No repair work or billable component replacements are performed without prior verbal or WhatsApp approval of the cost estimate by the client.
  • No Unpleasant Surprises: We adhere strictly to our transparent diagnostic policy. If a device is declared unrepairable due to severe board corrosion or cracked multi-layer PCB traces, only the agreed standard diagnostic inspection fee applies.

2. 90-Day Genuine Replacement Hardware Warranty

  • All new replacement components (laptop laptop screens, original keyboards, replacement batteries, NVMe SSDs, printer pick-up rollers, and thermal cooling fans) carry our comprehensive 90-Day Hardware Replacement Warranty.

  • Warranty does not cover subsequent accidental liquid spillage, physical drops, broken display glass, electrical surges (lightning strikes), or unauthorized third-party tampering after delivery.

3. Customer Data Backup Responsibility

  • While our technicians exercise extreme care to preserve all partition tables and operating systems, clients are encouraged to maintain an external backup of critical files before booking motherboard, BIOS re-flashing, or OS reinstallation repairs.
