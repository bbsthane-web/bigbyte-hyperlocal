Epson EcoTank Alternating Red Light Blinking: Waste Ink Sponge Reset

📍 Location: Manpada Lab, Thane West
⏱️ Turnaround: Same-Day (2 Hours)
🛠️ Diagnosis: Component-Level Bench Test

Reported Symptom

Power and ink lights blinking alternately, printer stopped midway with 'Service Required' error on desktop.

Bench Turnaround

Same-Day (2 Hours)

Technician Resolution

100% Component Fixed

Laboratory Inspection & Diagnostic Steps

  1. Interrogated internal printer mainboard EEPROM: read waste ink counter register at 100.8% capacity.
  2. Safely demounted rear waste ink containment box and extracted saturated waste ink felt absorber pads.
  3. Replaced with factory-grade multi-layer absorption sponge assembly to prevent ink overflow onto motherboard.
  4. Connected dedicated service diagnostic dongle and calibrated EEPROM counter back to 0.00%.
  5. Conducted automated printhead alignment and 5-stage CMYK test pattern to ensure nozzle sharpness.

💡 Diagnostic Outcome & Savings

Printer fully resurrected with crisp streak-free printing. Cost was less than 20% of buying a new machine.

Frequently Asked Questions for this Issue

Q: What causes the alternating red blinking lights on Epson L3110 / L3150 / L3250?

A: Every time the printer cleans its nozzles, ink is pumped into internal waste pads. When the safety counter hits maximum capacity, Epson locks the machine to prevent hazardous ink from leaking onto your table or electrical board.

Q: Is resetting the software counter enough without changing the sponge?

A: No! Just resetting software leaves the soaked sponge in place. Within weeks, excess ink overflows and short-circuits the main formatter logic board. We always replace or overhaul the physical sponges.
